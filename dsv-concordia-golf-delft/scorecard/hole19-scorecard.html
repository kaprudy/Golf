Description

Concordia Delft Golf is a charming 9-hole golf course located in the picturesque city of Delft, South Holland, Netherlands. Perfect for both beginners and seasoned players, this par-3 course is designed to provide a fun yet challenging experience with its well-manicured greens, varied hole layouts, and serene setting. Whether you're looking to sharpen your short game or enjoy a quick round, Concordia Golf offers an excellent opportunity to play amidst the beauty of the Dutch landscape. The course features top-notch practice facilities, including a driving range and putting green, ideal for golfers looking to improve their skills. After your round, relax in the welcoming clubhouse, where you can enjoy delicious refreshments and warm hospitality. Its convenient location near Delft makes it a great choice for locals and visitors exploring the South Holland region. Concordia Delft Golf delivers a laid-back, enjoyable golfing experience for all skill levels.
