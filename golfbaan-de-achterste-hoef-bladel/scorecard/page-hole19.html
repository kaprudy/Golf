Description

Golfbaan De Achterste Hoef is an 18-hole course in Bladel, North Brabant, open to golfers of all levels — beginners, recreational players, and professionals alike, seven days a week. The course sits within the Brabantse Kempen landscape, in the region known as 'de Acht Zaligheden', a short distance from Eindhoven. The surrounding woodland extends across the border into Belgium, reaching toward Arendonk, Turnhout, Mol, and Retie, giving the course a spacious, natural character. The facility includes extensive practice areas, a professional golf school, and a clubhouse with two function rooms and food and beverage service — everything expected of a modern golf park.
