About Open Golf Zandvoort

Open Golf Zandvoort is a 9-hole golf club in Zandvoort, The Netherlands, North Holland, Netherlands. The club boasts a 9-hole golf course situated amidst the dunes, offering views of the North Sea coastline. It features undulating fairways, strategically placed bunkers, and greens that require precision and strategy. With its coastal setting and links-style layout, the course provides a unique golfing experience that is highly appreciated by players. In addition to the golf course, Open Golf Zandvoort offers modern and comprehensive practice facilities. These include a driving range, putting greens, and bunker areas, allowing golfers to hone their skills and improve their game. The club also provides golf lessons and clinics for beginners as well as experienced players, making it an ideal place for both recreational and competitive golfers. Open Golf Zandvoort also features a clubhouse with excellent amenities. Golfers can relax and unwind in the comfortable lounge area, enjoy delicious cuisine in the restaurant, or simply take in the scenic views from the terrace.
