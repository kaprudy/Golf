Description

Golfbaan The Dunes is a public 9-hole links course in Zandvoort, North Holland, Netherlands, set among dunes and alongside the strand. Rated 4.0 by 50+ Hole19 golfers, the course plays through genuine links terrain with dunes shaping the layout throughout. A driving range and putting greens are available on site. The pro shop has been noted for being accommodating and pleasant. Brasserie The Links is open seven days a week, with views over the course and dunes, serving straightforward food and drinks.
