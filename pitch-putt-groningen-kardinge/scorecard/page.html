Description

Pitch & Putt Golf Groningen is an 18-hole golf course located in the vibrant city of Groningen, Netherlands. Perfect for both beginners and seasoned players, this course delivers a fun yet challenging experience with its thoughtfully designed layout. The compact course is ideal for honing your short game, making it a favorite among locals and golf tourists alike. Practice facilities are available to refine your swing before you hit the greens, and the welcoming atmosphere makes it easy for groups, families, or solo golfers to enjoy a great day out. After your round, head to the modern clubhouse, where you can relax and enjoy refreshments in a comfortable setting. Whether you're visiting Groningen or live nearby, Pitch & Putt Golf Groningen offers a unique golfing experience that blends strategy, skill, and fun. Book your round today and discover why this course is a must-visit destination for golf enthusiasts!
