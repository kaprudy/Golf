Welcome at Golfclub De Hoge Kleij.

For all information about bookings and greenfees, please contact the caddiemaster by phone:  +31 (0)33 461 69 44 (option 1) or by e-mail: caddiemaster@hogekleij.nl.

Visitors and member guests must book in advance via our caddiemaster.
Visitors will need a WHS handicap of 28,0 or lower and are welcome to play on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday. On Saturday and Sunday visitors are welcome after 15.00 hrs.

Please use the following link for all course information and the English version of our Local Rules: https://hogekleij.nl/de-baan/plaatselijke-regels/
