Description

Landgoed Ullerberg in Ermelo, Gelderland, Netherlands, is a hidden gem for golf enthusiasts. This unique 9-hole natural course blends seamlessly into its picturesque heathland surroundings, offering an exceptional golfing experience. The course features a mix of challenging holes, with the longest stretching 423 meters and the shortest at 106 meters. For those playing 18 holes, the total length is 5,130 meters for men and 4,616 meters for women. Landgoed Ullerberg is ideal for both local players and golf tourists seeking an authentic Dutch golfing experience. The natural greens and heather-lined fairways create a serene atmosphere, perfect for a relaxing round of golf. Although compact, the course is meticulously maintained, ensuring excellent playability year-round. Practice your swing at the on-site facilities before enjoying the warm hospitality of the clubhouse. Whether you're sharpening your skills or simply soaking in the tranquil environment, Landgoed Ullerberg promises a memorable visit. Experience one of Gelderland's most scenic courses.
