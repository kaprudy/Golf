 History
 Kasteel Engelenburg Golf Course, located in picturesque Brummen, has a rich history dating back to the early 20th century. The golf course is part of Landgoed Groot Engelenburg, a historic estate dating back to the 17th century. 
 The origins of Kasteel Engelenburg Golf Course can be traced back to 1988, when the golf course was built on the grounds of Groot Engelenburg Estate. Course design was entrusted to renowned golf course architect Donald Steel. Steel created a challenging 9-hole golf course that blends harmoniously with the estate s beautiful landscape. The course offers golfers a unique experience amidst ancient trees, ponds and rolling hills. 
 In subsequent years, Kasteel Engelenburg Golf Course underwent several renovations and improvements to optimize the playing experience. The estate on which the golf course is located also has a rich history. It once served as a country retreat for noble families and has undergone several transformations over the centuries. 
 Today, Kasteel Engelenburg Golf Course is known for its beautiful surroundings and challenging golf opportunities. Golfers of all skill levels can enjoy the atmosphere of this historic estate while improving their golf skills. The combination of history, nature and sports makes Kasteel Engelenburg Golf Course a unique destination for golf enthusiasts.
