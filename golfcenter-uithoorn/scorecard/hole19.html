Description

Golfcenter Uithoorn is a public 9-hole par-3 course in De Kwakel, North Holland, Netherlands, playing over 764 meters. The course is open to all golfers, making it a practical option for those building their short game or playing a quick round. A driving range is available on site for warming up or working on your swing, and lessons can be booked with one of the on-site pros. A golf shop is also located at the driving range.
