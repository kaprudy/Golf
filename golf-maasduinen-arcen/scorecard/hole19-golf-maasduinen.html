Description

Golf Maasduinen is an inviting 9-hole golf course located in the serene countryside of Arcen, Limburg, Netherlands. Known for its accessible and welcoming atmosphere, this course features a mix of par 3 and par 4 holes, thoughtfully designed with water hazards and bunkers to challenge golfers of all levels. Whether you’re a seasoned player with an NGF handicap of 54 or a beginner with course permission, you'll find a rewarding experience here. The course offers unlimited play with an affordable day pass, making it a favorite among locals and visitors alike. Golf Maasduinen also boasts free practice facilities, perfect for sharpening your skills before heading out on the course. After your round, relax at the cozy clubhouse, complete with a terrace where you can soak in the beautiful surroundings. Discover an unforgettable golfing experience in the heart of Limburg today.
