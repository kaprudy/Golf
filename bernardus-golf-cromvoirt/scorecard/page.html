Golf

Challenging to play. Easy to love.

Bernardus Golf is a challenging 18-hole championship golf course, designed by American architect Kyle Phillips, who was also responsible for the design of world famous courses like Kingsbarns, The Grove and Yas Links.

Bernardus Golf was built as a heathland golf course, which makes it accessible to any golfer. The numerous bunkers are laid out beautifully and the tricky water hazards seamlessly blend in with the background of dunes, heath and gorse. A day at Bernardus gives you the ultimate feeling of peace, quiet and relaxation. Both on the course and the practice facilities; driving range, short-game area and putting greens.

Driving Range

Make sure you have a good warm-up before you step onto the course, by hitting some Titleist balls or practice your swing as often as you want. Bernardus features a driving range and a grass tee. Enjoy the front end of the range with others (and have a cup of coffee at the bar) or find more peace and tranquility on the other side of the range on the grass tee troughout the year.


Putting green

Bernardus features two putting greens, that have the same speed as our greens on the course. This way, you are most likely to see immediate results during your next round.


Short-game area

The short-game area is nicely situated in front of an old church and offers training opportunities for every obstacle you may encounter on our course. From bunkers, to varying grass heights and sloping surfaces. This practice area offers an ideal setting before you start your round.


On course service

From a refreshment, a snack or other catering service to our equipment service, we’ve got you covered during your entire day at Bernardus.
