About Golfbaan Sluispolder

Golfbaan Sluispolder is a golf club located in Alkmaar, Netherlands. The club boasts a challenging 27-hole course set amidst the polder landscape of North Holland. The course has a mix of open fields and wooded areas, with water features scattered throughout. The club also has a driving range, practice facilities, a well-stocked pro shop, and a restaurant serving delicious meals to players and visitors alike.

About this course

The Golfbaan Sluispolder course is an 18-hole par 72 course.

It measures 5,865 metres from the Yellow tees.

The scorecard has 4 par 3s, 10 par 4s and 4 par 5s.

The longest hole is the 1st (par 5, 480 metres), while the shortest is the 15th (par 3, 129 metres).

The hardest hole by stroke index is the 12th (par 4, 394 metres, stroke index 1).
