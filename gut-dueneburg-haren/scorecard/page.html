About Golfclub Gut-Düneburg

Golfclub Gut-Düneburg is an 18-hole golf club in Haren (Ems), Lower Saxony, Germany. Set within a 300-hectare parkland, the course integrates natural elements like moorlands, heaths, and woodlands, offering a challenging layout with strategic risk-reward scenarios.

Course details

  • Number of holes: 18
  • Par: 72
  • Tees available: 4

About this course

The Gut Duneburg course is an 18-hole par 72 course.

The scorecard has 4 par 3s, 10 par 4s and 4 par 5s.

The hardest hole by stroke index is the 6th (par 4, stroke index 1).

The Weiss tees have a course rating of 72.9 and a slope rating of 136.

The Gelb tees have a course rating of 71.8 and a slope rating of 133.

The Blau (L) tees have a course rating of 73.6 and a slope rating of 130.

The Rot (L) tees have a course rating of 72.6 and a slope rating of 128.
