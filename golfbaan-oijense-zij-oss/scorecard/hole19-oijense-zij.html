Description

Golfbaan Oijense Zij Oss is a 9-hole, par-3 layout in Oss, North Brabant, Netherlands, measuring 729 meters in total. At 729 meters across nine par-3 holes, the course is compact and precise — every hole demands accuracy over distance. It is a practical choice for players building their short game or looking for a focused round. The pro shop, Golfshop Oijense Zij Oss, stocks clubs, shoes, bags, trolleys, tees, balls, gloves, and accessories, with a fitting service available. A driving range is on site and open for demo club testing, alongside a dedicated practice green. PGA-Professional instruction covers individual lessons, group lessons, beginner lessons, advanced lessons, and quick courses (Snelcursus GVB).
