Description

Golfbaan Weesp is a centrally located 18-hole course in Weesp, North Holland, positioned between Amsterdam Zuid and het Gooi. The course sits alongside the Amsterdam–Rhine Canal, where large inland vessels pass as you play — a distinctive backdrop to your round. The layout combines par 3 and par 4 holes in a varied and challenging mix. The par 3 holes range from 90 to 160 metres, averaging 125 metres each — comparable to par 3 holes on full-length courses and notably longer than those on a typical short course. Access is straightforward: the course lies directly on the N236 provincial road, just five minutes from both the A1 and A9 motorways.
