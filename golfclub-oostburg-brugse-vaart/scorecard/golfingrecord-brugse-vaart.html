About Golfclub Oostburg

Golfclub Oostburg, also known as Domein De Brugse Vaart, is an 18-hole championship course located in Oostburg, Netherlands. The club's impressive clubhouse, reminiscent of the White House, offers a warm and familial atmosphere to both members and guests.

Course details

  • Number of holes: 18
  • Par: 73
  • Tees available: 4

About this course

The De Brugse Vaart course is an 18-hole par 73 course.

The scorecard has 5 par 3s, 7 par 4s and 6 par 5s.

The hardest hole by stroke index is the 5th (par 4, stroke index 1).

The Geel tees have a course rating of 73.8 and a slope rating of 139.
