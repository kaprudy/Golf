Description

Golfbaan 9par3 Noordeloos is a charming 9-hole par-3 golf course located in the picturesque Noordeloos village in South Holland, Netherlands. This course, officially recognized with an EGA-handicap rating, offers a unique challenge for golfers of all skill levels. Each hole is compact yet thoughtfully designed, making it the perfect setting for honing your short game skills. The tricky layouts and strategic obstacles ensure an exciting round for both beginners and experienced players. The course is nestled in a serene countryside setting, providing a peaceful escape for local golfers and visitors alike. Practice facilities are available, offering an excellent opportunity to refine your swing before hitting the course. After your game, unwind at the welcoming clubhouse, where you can enjoy refreshments and connect with fellow golfers. Whether you're a seasoned player looking for a short-game challenge or a newcomer just starting your golf journey, Golfbaan 9par3 Noordeloos is a must-visit destination. Play golf, relax, and take in the scenic beauty of South Holland at this delightful 9-hole course.
