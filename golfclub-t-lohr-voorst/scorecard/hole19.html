Description

Golfclub 't Lohr is an 18-hole short course in Voorst, Gelderland, set within an attractive landscape and supported by extensive practice facilities. Despite the shorter distances — holes range from 33 to 78 metres — the layout incorporates bunkers, water features, hills, trees, and shrubs, giving it the character of a full-length course. The challenge is genuine; only the yardages differ. For more experienced golfers, a dedicated 'long 9 holes' runs along the outer ring, stretching up to 123 metres. This loop also makes it possible to obtain an NGF handicap at 't Lohr.
