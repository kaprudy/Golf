About Hitland

The golf club "Hitland" is located in Nieuwerkerk a/d IJssel in the Netherlands. It is a golf course situated in the scenic Hitland recreational area. The club offers an 18-hole championship golf course surrounded by water and nature, providing a challenging and enjoyable golfing experience for players of all skill levels. In addition to the main course, Hitland Golf Club also features a 9-hole Par-3 course, which is perfect for beginners or those who want to work on their short game. The club has excellent practice facilities, including a driving range, putting green, and chipping area, allowing golfers to hone their skills before hitting the course. Hitland Golf Club has a welcoming and friendly atmosphere, encouraging both members and non-members to enjoy the sport. They organize various golf events, tournaments, and lessons for all ages and abilities. The club also has a well-equipped pro shop with a wide range of golf equipment, apparel, and accessories. The clubhouse at Hitland Golf Club offers a comfortable environment for golfers to relax and socialize. It features a restaurant and terrace with views of the golf course, where golfers can enjoy delicious meals and drinks after a round of golf.

About this course

The Hitland course is an 18-hole par 71 course.

It measures 5,819 metres from the Yellow tees.

The scorecard has 5 par 3s, 9 par 4s and 4 par 5s.

The longest hole is the 13th (par 5, 472 metres), while the shortest is the 9th (par 3, 128 metres).

The hardest hole by stroke index is the 12th (par 4, 382 metres, stroke index 1).
