About Golfvereniging Reymerswael

Golfvereniging Reymerswael is an 18-hole golf club in Rilland, Zeeland, Netherlands. The course features undulating fairways, low vegetation, and occasional dune tops, providing a challenging experience with views of the Westerschelde and the Scheldt-Rhine Canal. The ever-present wind adds an extra layer of difficulty, making it a unique destination for golfers seeking both beauty and challenge.

Course details

  • Number of holes: 18
  • Par: 71
  • Tees available: 5

About this course

The Reymerswael course is an 18-hole par 71 course.

The scorecard has 4 par 3s, 11 par 4s and 3 par 5s.

The hardest hole by stroke index is the 13th (par 5, stroke index 1).
